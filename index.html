AtlasOps Intelligence Platform

End-to-end cloud analytics platform built to simulate enterprise operational intelligence across multiple U.S. regions. Architected using Azure Blob for raw ingestion, Snowflake for ELT and star schema modeling, and Power BI for multi-layer reporting — delivered as a structured Power BI Service App.

Business Objective

  • Centralize performance across region → state → city → warehouse.
  • Identify drivers of late orders and bottlenecks.
  • Measure department and employee impact on errors and throughput.
  • Detect anomalies and forecast near-term demand.

Architecture
